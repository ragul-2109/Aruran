About Aruran Agro Solution

Dedicated to Modern Livestock & Dairy Productivity.

Based in Thulukkanur, Attur (Salem District), Aruran Agro Solution is an agriculture and livestock-centric business founded to bridge the gap between traditional dairy farming and modern agricultural mechanization.

Green fodder shortage and labor scarcity are the biggest challenges faced by dairy farmers today. We provide high-quality fermented corn silage that maintains year-round milk output, along with robust grass/chaff cutters and reliable milking machines designed for Indian dairy conditions.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to help you make an informed decision for your farm.

How long can the sealed silage bags be stored?

Our silage is packed in heavy-duty 7-layer UV-stabilized airtight 50kg bags. As long as the bag remains sealed and undamaged, it stays fresh and highly nutritious for 12 to 18 months. Once opened, we recommend using the bag within 3 to 5 days.

What electric power phase is needed for the Chaff Cutter?

We provide chaff cutters in both Single-Phase (220V domestic or farm line) and Three-Phase configurations. The 2HP to 3HP models run effortlessly on regular single-phase farm electricity, so you do not need a separate commercial connection.

Do you provide doorstep delivery to our village?

Yes! We arrange direct mini-truck, pickup, or parcel transport delivery across Attur, Salem, Namakkal, Kallakurichi, Dharmapuri, Perambalur, and all across Tamil Nadu right to your farm gate. Contact us for freight details.

Is the milking machine safe for cows? Will it cause infection?

Our milking machines use medical food-grade silicone liners and a factory-calibrated 60:40 pulsation cycle that simulates a calf's natural suckling action. It massages the teats and prevents congestion or mastitis, ensuring complete udder health.
